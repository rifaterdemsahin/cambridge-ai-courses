Who We're Built For

"Hands-on AI certification prep" means something different depending on who's reading it. Here's how we segment our market, and the dedicated page we built for each one.

One Product, Five Perceptions

Cambridge AI Courses sells the same underlying thing — exam-mapped curriculum, hands-on labs, and certification outcomes across Claude, Azure AI, and NVIDIA GenAI. But no single ideal customer profile (ICP) captures who actually buys it. A parent isn't evaluating TensorRT-LLM deployment depth. An NHS board isn't asking whether the course is "fun." Each segment below filters our pitch through its own goal, and reacts to a different proof point.

SegmentWho They ArePrimary GoalHow They Perceive Us
Individual Professionals Engineers & career-switchers, self-funded Get certified, get hired or promoted A credential + portfolio, measured in career ROI
Parents & Families Parents of children ages 6–16 Safe, confidence-building AI education A guided activity, measured in enthusiasm & safety
Corporate L&D Buyers HR / L&D managers, cohorts of 50–300 Upskill the workforce at predictable cost A line item, measured in cost-per-seat & adoption
Academic Institutions University partnership & curriculum offices Embed certification into degree programs An employability differentiator, measured in outcomes
Enterprise & Public Sector C-level executives, NHS-style boards Governance, risk, and compliance coverage An auditable control, not a training expense
