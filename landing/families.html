Privacy & Safety for Families

This program is built around parental consent and a private-by-default workspace for your child. Full legal terms are still in review, but here's the plain-language summary.

✅

Parent-Only Signup

Only a parent or legal guardian can create the account and must confirm consent before enrollment. Your child never logs in with their own email address.

🔒

Private by Default

Your child's projects are only visible via a share link you or they choose to send — never publicly searchable, browsable, or indexed.

👀

You Can See Everything

The Parent Dashboard shows every project and every active share link — nothing your child creates is hidden from you, and you can delete their account and data at any time.
