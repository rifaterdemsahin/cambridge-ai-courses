B2B Sales

Cold Email & Sales Strategy

Target & Objective

This template targets L&D directors, Heads of Talent, and CTOs at mid-to-large enterprises (300+ employees) who are evaluating AI upskilling. The single objective of the first email is a 20-minute discovery call — not a close. Program pricing lives in the Pricing & Scheduling Model.

Subject Line Options (A/B test)

  • "AI certification for your 300-person team — a quick idea"
  • "[Company]'s AI upskilling, without the guesswork"
  • "Quick question about [Company]'s AI training roadmap"

Cold Email Template

Hi [First Name],

I'll keep this short — I know AI training pitches are landing in your inbox weekly right now.

We run Cambridge AI's corporate certification program: hands-on, certification-backed AI training (Claude, Azure AI, and NVIDIA GenAI tracks) built for teams of 300+, not generic e-learning seats. Every employee finishes with a verifiable certification and a real project they built, not a completion badge.

A few things clients like [Comparable Company / Industry Peer] have found useful:

  • A cohort structure sized and scheduled around your team's actual availability — not a fixed course calendar
  • Bespoke tracks mapped to your tech stack (e.g. if you're an Azure shop, we weight the curriculum accordingly)
  • A single dashboard for your L&D team to track completion, certification pass rates, and skills coverage across the whole cohort

Would it be worth 20 minutes to see whether this fits [Company]'s upskilling plans for this year? Happy to work around your schedule — here's a link: [Calendar Link].

Best,
[Your Name]
Cambridge AI Courses · Corporate Programs
[email] · [phone] · [LinkedIn]

Program Expectations (reference block for the call, or a follow-up attachment)

  • Cohort size: Scales from a single 6-person pilot team up to a full 300-person rollout, run in parallel cohorts of 6–16 learners for hands-on quality.
  • Format: Blended — live instructor-led workshops plus self-paced Digital Library modules between sessions.
  • Duration: 6–10 weeks per track, matching the public Claude, Azure, and NVIDIA curricula.
  • Delivery: On-site at the client's offices, at a partner venue, or fully remote — see the pricing model for venue-based surcharges.
  • Assessment: Every learner completes a hands-on capstone project and sits a certification exam; pass rates are reported back to the client's L&D team.

Certification Benefits (for the buyer)

  • Verifiable, third-party-recognized credentials employees can list on LinkedIn — improves retention and internal mobility signaling.
  • A skills-coverage report the client can use for compliance, tenders, or client-facing credibility (e.g. proving AI competency to their own customers).
  • Reduced onboarding time for AI tooling already in use, since employees graduate having built a real project on the company's actual stack.

Bespoke Options

  • Stack-matched curriculum: Weighting toward Claude, Azure AI, or NVIDIA GenAI (or a custom blend) based on the client's existing infrastructure.
  • Private capstone projects: Built against the client's own (sandboxed) data and use cases instead of generic exercises.
  • Custom scheduling: Weekday-only, evening, or weekend-intensive formats — priced per the dynamic model in the Pricing & Scheduling Model.
  • Executive briefing add-on: A condensed session for leadership, distinct from the hands-on employee track (see the Enterprise Pitch outline).

Follow-Up Sequence

Follow-up 1 · Day 4 (no reply)

Light Bump

Subject: Re: [original subject]

"Wanted to make sure this didn't get buried — happy to send a one-page overview of the program instead if that's easier to route internally. Let me know either way!"

Follow-up 2 · Day 10 (no reply)

Value-Add, Low Pressure

Subject: A relevant resource for [Company]

Shares a short case study or benchmark stat (e.g. certification pass rate, average time-to-productivity improvement) with no explicit ask beyond "thought this might be useful regardless of timing."

Follow-up 3 · Day 21 (no reply)

Breakup Email

Subject: Should I close the loop here?

"I don't want to keep cluttering your inbox — I'll assume the timing isn't right and step back. If AI upskilling comes back onto the roadmap, just reply here and I'll pick this straight back up."
