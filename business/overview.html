Three Ways We Work With Organizations

Corporate L&D Cohorts

We run cohort-based certification training for company teams of roughly 6–300 people, across the Claude, Azure AI, and NVIDIA GenAI tracks. Programs include a shared dashboard for tracking completion and certification outcomes across a cohort. Learn more →

Academic Institution Partnerships

We're actively reaching out to universities interested in embedding our certification tracks into existing degree or executive-education programs, with external moderation of capstone assessments. These conversations are at the outreach stage — we'll name specific institutional partnerships publicly once an agreement is actually signed. Learn more →

Enterprise & Public Sector

For larger organizations, including public-sector boards, we frame certification as a governance and risk-management tool: an auditable record of who is trained, to what standard, and when. Learn more →
