Internal Audit

Assets vs. Liabilities Matrix

What we can currently defend as real and verified, next to what's still an open risk or unresolved gap. Internal use — not for public distribution.

How to Read This

Asset = independently verifiable, or a genuine risk-reduction already shipped (a disclaimer, a noindex tag, a written policy). Liability = something a prospect, journalist, regulator, or certifying body could challenge us on today. This is a snapshot as of 4 August 2026 — update it as items move from one column to the other.

Credibility & Naming

Assets

  • Sitewide disclaimer on all 16 pages stating we are independent and not affiliated with the University of Cambridge.
  • Every mention of Cambridge Judge, ARU, Huddersfield, and the NHS now explicitly labeled prospective / no agreement in place.

Liabilities

  • No signed partnership or MOU exists with any named institution — zero confirmed revenue or relationship from this list today.
  • "Cambridge AI Courses" as a brand name still invites the affiliation assumption the disclaimer exists to correct; a name change was not evaluated in this pass.

Proof & Trust Signals

Assets

  • Rifat's Claude Certified Architect – Professional credential (score 905) is independently verifiable via Credly and linked from the site.
  • Current cohort (Sude, Brian, Bayo) referenced by name and honestly, with no fabricated quotes attributed to them.
  • 94% pass-rate stat is now footnoted as a target, not presented as audited historical data.
  • Guarantee terms are written out in full on terms.html, not just implied in FAQ copy.

Liabilities

  • Marianna Nechypor has no equivalent published, verifiable credential — her bio is not independently backed the way Rifat's is.
  • No employer logos exist to display; TRUST-02's logo ask is unmet.
  • No real learner testimonials collected yet — the drafted quotes in onboarding copy are explicitly marked as unpublishable placeholders.
  • 94% is still an estimate, not a measured number; the underlying "audited pass-rate data" doesn't exist yet because the program is too young to have it.

Confidentiality

Assets

  • Internal sales docs (pricing formula, outreach template, partnership strategy, enterprise pitch, onboarding copy) are noindexed and disallowed in robots.txt.
  • Public "Business Hub" nav link now points to the sanitized overview.html, not the raw internal hub.
  • Written plan (CONFIDENTIALITY-PLAN.md) documenting real password-protection options given static hosting.

Liabilities

  • Noindex is not access control — every internal doc is still fetchable by direct URL or by cloning the public repo.
  • This matrix page itself contains sensitive risk data and is noindexed but, for the same reason, not actually protected.
  • Real protection (private repo, split hosting, or an access-controlled host) has not been implemented, only scoped.

Pricing & Conversion

Assets

  • Public pricing.html with transparent $499/track pricing, inclusions, and a three-track comparison table.
  • Lower-friction CTAs ("Download Full Syllabus," "Book a 15-Min Advisory Call") replacing "Enroll Now" sitewide.

Liabilities

  • No live payment processor — every "enroll" or "syllabus" action still routes to a demo form that only fires a JS alert(), not a real email or backend.
  • No refund or dispute infrastructure exists to back the 14-day refund language now published in terms.html.

Young Explorers / Minors

Assets

  • Parental-consent, privacy, and safety disclosures now exist on both the internal onboarding doc and the public families landing page.
  • Two fabricated claims caught and removed before ever reaching a live page (fake Cambridge affiliation, fake review count).

Liabilities

  • None of the COPPA / UK GDPR Children's Code language has been reviewed by qualified legal counsel — explicitly flagged as draft, but still a real gap before this track can accept a real signup.
  • Footer "Privacy Policy" link is still a placeholder ("#") — a real privacy policy is a prerequisite for a track that collects data on children, and it does not exist yet.

Content Depth

Assets

  • Case-study template with a written consent checklist, so a future case study can't be published without the learner's sign-off.

Liabilities

  • Zero real case studies exist yet — the template is unpopulated.
  • Footer "Honor Code" link is still a placeholder ("#") with no underlying document.

Highest-Priority Liabilities Right Now

  1. No real Privacy Policy, despite the Young Explorers track collecting data on children — blocks that track's public launch.
  2. No legal review of terms.html or the minors' compliance copy — both are currently good-faith drafts standing in for reviewed policy.
  3. Internal sales docs are unindexed but not actually access-controlled — still a live confidentiality exposure until a hosting change lands.
