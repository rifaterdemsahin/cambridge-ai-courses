Compliance & Safety Disclosures (Draft — Pending Legal Review)

Status

This section is a first draft to unblock design and copy work. It must be reviewed by qualified legal counsel (UK GDPR / Children's Code, and COPPA if we accept US signups) before the Young Explorers track accepts a real signup.

Parental Consent (shown at signup, before payment details)

  • Only a parent or legal guardian may create the account; the checkbox above must be checked to proceed, and we log the timestamp and IP of that consent alongside the account.
  • The account is registered to the parent/guardian's name and email — the child never logs in with their own email address, and we do not collect one for children under 13.
  • Parents can revoke consent and permanently delete their child's account and all associated project data at any time from the Parent Dashboard, or by emailing info@pexabo.com.

Age-Appropriate Privacy Disclosure (plain-language summary for parents)

  • What we collect: the child's first name and age band (not full name, not date of birth), their project work, and usage activity needed to run the Digital Library and track progress. No location data, no third-party advertising trackers on child-facing pages.
  • Published Portal is private-by-default: a project is only visible to people who have the specific share link the parent or child chooses to send — it is never publicly searchable or indexed, and there is no public discovery/browse feature exposing one child's work to strangers.
  • No behavioral advertising to children: we do not run targeted ads on any page a logged-in child account can reach, and we do not sell or share children's data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • Data retention: if a trial or membership lapses, project data is retained for 90 days (so a family can resume) and then deleted, unless the parent requests earlier deletion.

Safety Guidelines

  • All AI interactions in the Explorers (6–10) and Builders (11–16) tracks run through the same content-safety filtering used in our adult tracks, tuned to reject age-inappropriate outputs rather than merely flagging them.
  • Human moderation review applies to anything published to a Portal before a share link becomes active for the under-13 age band.
  • Parents can access everything their child has created and every share link that's active, at any time, from the Parent Dashboard — there is no child-only content a parent cannot see.
  • A visible "Report" option is available next to any shared project or shared link, routed to a human, not an automated queue only.

Welcome Email Sequence: Free Trial → Paid Subscription

Seven emails across the 14-day trial, timed to build a habit early, showcase the Digital Library and Published Portal as the core "aha" moments, and make the trial-to-paid conversion feel like a natural next step rather than a hard sell.

Email 1 · Sent immediately

Welcome & First Login

Subject: "Welcome to Cambridge AI 🎉 Here's [Child's Name]'s first mission"

Confirms sign-up, gives a direct login link, and sets expectation that the first project takes under 10 minutes. Introduces the Digital Library as "where every lesson lives" and shows a screenshot of the first unlocked project.

Email 2 · Day 2

The Digital Library, Unlocked

Subject: "500+ projects are waiting in [Child's Name]'s library"

Tours the Digital Library by age band, highlights 3 popular starter projects, and nudges parents to pick the next one together. Goal: second login within 48 hours, since day-2 return is the strongest predictor of trial conversion.

Email 3 · Day 4

Introducing the Published Portal

Subject: "[Child's Name] can publish their first project today"

Explains the Published Portal as a safe, private-by-default showcase for finished work, and walks through how to publish and share a link with grandparents or classmates. This is the emotional hook of the sequence — pride of ownership.

Email 4 · Day 7

Halfway Progress Recap

Subject: "[Child's Name]'s Week 1 progress report"

Personalized recap of lessons completed and projects published (or a gentle nudge if activity has been low, with a "pick up where you left off" link). Reinforces the parent dashboard as an ongoing feature of paid membership.

Email 5 · Day 10

What Unlocks With Paid Membership

Subject: "4 days left — here's what stays unlocked"

First explicit mention of the trial ending. Frames Paid Membership as continuity, not a new purchase: unlimited Digital Library access, unlimited Published Portal projects, and monthly progress reports, from £19/month. Includes one more testimonial.

Email 6 · Day 12

Trial Ending Soon

Subject: "[Child's Name]'s trial ends in 2 days"

Direct, low-pressure reminder with a one-click "Continue for £19/month" button. Reassures that all completed projects and Published Portal work are saved permanently either way.

Email 7 · Day 14

Trial Ends — Convert or Pause

Subject: "It's decision day for [Child's Name]'s AI journey"

Final email: confirms Paid Membership starts automatically unless paused, restates the value of the Digital Library and Published Portal, and offers a "talk to us" link for parents who have questions before committing.
