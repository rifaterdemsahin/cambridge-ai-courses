Finance & Operations

Pricing & Scheduling Model

Cost Inputs

  • Base venue rate: £100 / hour
  • Weekend day multiplier: Saturday ×1.25, Sunday ×1.35 (applied to the hourly venue rate)
  • Lead-day surcharge: £50 / hour for a setup or prep day taken the day before the event (billed at 50% of the standard rate, since no live session runs)
  • Trainer & facilitation fee: £600 flat per day (lead instructor + assistant), +20% on Saturday/Sunday sessions
  • Materials & certification fee: £45 per participant — covers Digital Library access, workbooks, and the certification exam voucher

Dynamic Pricing Formula

Cohorts run from 6 to 16 participants to preserve hands-on quality — above 16 we split into parallel cohorts rather than enlarging one room. Because venue and trainer costs are fixed per session, the per-participant price falls naturally as cohort size grows, with no manual discount table to maintain.

Venue Cost

V = (H × £100 × D) + L

Where H = session hours, D = day multiplier (Weekday 1.0 / Saturday 1.25 / Sunday 1.35), and L = lead-day surcharge (£50 × lead-day hours, or £0 if no lead day is booked).

Total Program Fee

F(n) = V + T + (M × n)

Where T = trainer & facilitation fee (£600/day, +20% on weekends), M = £45 materials/certification fee per head, and n = cohort size (6–16).

Per-Participant Price

P(n) = F(n) / n

The client-facing price per seat. Larger cohorts absorb the fixed venue and trainer costs across more people, so P(n) decreases as n rises from 6 to 16 — a built-in volume incentive without discretionary discounting.

Worked Example A — Weekday Workshop (8 hours, no lead day)

V = 8 × £100 × 1.0 = £800. T = £600. F(n) = £1,400 + (£45 × n).

Budget Proposal Template

For a client-facing budget line item, present the formula outputs as a simple three-line proposal:

Venue & Facilities: V (per session, per the formula above)
Facilitation: T (per session, weekday or weekend rate)
Materials, Digital Library Access & Certification: M × n
Total Program Investment: F(n), or P(n) per participant for headcount-based budgeting
