Content & Proof

Learner Case Study Template

Template Only — No Real Data Below

This is a structure to fill in once we have a completed learner willing to share their story, not a published case study. Every bracketed field must be replaced with real, learner-reviewed information, and the learner must give explicit written consent before anything below is published, even in anonymized form.

Before You Publish: Consent Checklist

  • Learner has reviewed the final draft, including the anonymized details, and approved it in writing (email is fine).
  • Learner has confirmed how they want to be identified: full name, first name only, initials, or fully anonymous (e.g. "a Corporate L&D learner, Azure track").
  • Any employer named has approved that mention, if the case study implies a specific company.
  • Reported scores/metrics are pulled directly from our platform records, not the learner's self-report, and a second person has checked the numbers before publishing.

Template Fields

1. Learner Profile (anonymized)

Identifier[Full name / first name / initials / "Anonymous" — per learner's stated preference]
Role & background[e.g. "Backend engineer, 4 years' experience, no prior LLM/AI tooling exposure"]
Segment[Individual Professional / Corporate L&D / Academic / Enterprise — see Audiences]
Track[Claude Specialist / Azure AI Engineer / NVIDIA GenAI Developer]
Cohort[e.g. "Spring 2026 cohort"]

2. Before — Starting Point

[Describe their skill level and specific gap in their own or the instructor's words, at a level of detail they've approved — e.g. "Could call the Claude API but had never implemented tool use or handled streaming responses in production."]

3. The Program

  • Duration completed: [actual weeks, from platform records]
  • Capstone project: [what they actually built, one or two sentences]
  • Notable obstacle & how they worked through it: [optional but strong — a specific, real sticking point, not a generic "they worked hard"]

4. After — Verified Outcome

Capstone result[e.g. "100% key results, verified [date]"]
Certification exam result[Pass/score, from the certifying body — only include if the learner has shared and approved it; do not publish exam scores without explicit permission, as this may violate the certifying body's candidate agreement]
Career/role change (if any)[e.g. "Promoted to Senior Engineer, cited certification in review" — only if learner confirms this in writing and is comfortable naming it]

5. Learner Quote (optional, verbatim, approved)

[Insert the learner's own words, unedited beyond minor grammar cleanup they've approved. Do not paraphrase into marketing voice — a slightly rough, specific quote reads as more credible than a polished one.]

Publishing Checklist

  • Cross-reference against Terms & Conditions and our data-handling commitments before naming an employer or publishing a score.
  • Store the signed/emailed consent alongside the published case study internally, not just the published text — if a learner later asks us to take it down, we need to be able to find and honor that quickly.
  • Once published, link it from the relevant audience landing page rather than only this Business Hub.
