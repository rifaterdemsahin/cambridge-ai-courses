The site currently lives at `rifaterdemsahin.github.io/cambridge-ai-courses`. This is a list of candidate domains for a proper custom domain, covering both a UK-market angle (`.co.uk`) and a global/tech angle (`.com`, `.ai`, `.io`). Availability was checked live via WHOIS on **2026-08-04** — re-check before purchasing, since domains can be registered at any time.

## Recommended top picks

| Domain | Why |
|---|---|
| **cambridgeaicourses.com** ✅ | Exact brand match to the current name — clearest, no rebrand needed |
| **cambridgeaicourses.co.uk** ✅ | Same brand, UK-market signal for local SEO and trust |
| **cambridgeaiacademy.com** ✅ | Slightly shorter, "academy" reads well for a course provider, still brand-safe |

Buying the `.com` and `.co.uk` of whichever name you pick (and 301-redirecting one to the other) is the standard move — it stops competitors or squatters grabbing the other TLD later.

## ⚠️ Naming caution: "Cambridge"

"Cambridge" is closely associated with the University of Cambridge and Cambridge Assessment, both of which are active about protecting the name in education-related branding. Using it as a standalone or leading brand element (as opposed to purely descriptive "AI courses in Cambridge") carries some trademark/passing-off risk in the UK. This isn't a reason to avoid it outright — plenty of independent Cambridge-based businesses trade under the town name — but it's worth a quick UK IPO trademark search before committing budget to a domain and printed materials.
