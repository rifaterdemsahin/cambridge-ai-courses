3. Exam-Voucher Reimbursement Guarantee

This is the guarantee referenced on our About & FAQ page, stated here in full:

What's Covered

If you complete all hands-on capstone key results for your track with a verified 100% score on our platform, we cover the cost of your official Microsoft or NVIDIA certification exam voucher, up to the standard list price set by that certification body.

  • Eligibility window: the capstone must be completed, and the 100% verification confirmed by our platform, within the active enrollment period for that track.
  • Verification: "100% score" means every key result in the capstone checklist for your track is marked complete and passes our automated and/or instructor-reviewed checks — not self-reported completion.
  • Exam bodies covered: currently Microsoft (Azure AI Engineer, AI-102) and NVIDIA (GenAI Developer). The Anthropic Claude track's certification does not currently carry a separate paid exam voucher, so this guarantee applies to the Azure and NVIDIA tracks.
  • How reimbursement works: once eligibility is confirmed, we issue or reimburse the official exam voucher directly through the certifying body's standard process; we do not pay cash in lieu of the voucher.
  • Retakes: this guarantee covers your first sitting of the official exam. Retake fees after a failed attempt are the student's responsibility unless otherwise agreed in writing.
  • Limits: one voucher per student per track. We reserve the right to decline reimbursement in cases of suspected fraud, plagiarized capstone work, or misuse of sandbox credentials.
