Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about enrollment, hardware access, and certification target metrics.

Do I need a GPU to take the NVIDIA Course?

No. We provide virtualized container access to NVIDIA H100 GPUs hosted in our private sandbox cloud. All compiler and server operations run directly in this environment.

What is the cost structure?

Each individual track is $499, which includes all sandbox cloud credits, api keys, and examination review vouchers. Enterprise team discounts are available.

Are the certification exam costs included?

Yes! If you successfully complete all hands-on capstone key results with a verified 100% score on our platform, we cover your Microsoft or NVIDIA official exam voucher. See the full guarantee terms & conditions →
